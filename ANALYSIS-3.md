# What to type instead of "Chopin style"

**47 names this catalogue used to carry in its style prompts, and the words that replaced them.**

Suno's style field doesn't accept artist names. A prompt that leans on one — *Chopin style*, *Bruno Mars style*, *Eno-style ambient* — asks the model for something it has been told not to give. Until this release, 47 of the 564 recipes in this repo did exactly that. This page is the fix, and the method behind it, so you can apply it to your own prompts.

## The method: a name is a shortcut for four things

When someone writes *Chopin style*, they don't mean the man. They mean a bundle the name stands for. Unpack the bundle and you get words the model can actually use:

1. **Era** — when the sound was made. *Romantic-era*, *1970s*, *late-18th-century*.
2. **Place** — where it was made. *Viennese*, *Addis Ababa*, *Parisian*, *Detroit*.
3. **Technique** — how it was made. *tape loops*, *process music*, *hot-club swing*, *spectralist*.
4. **Setting** — where it was heard. *salon*, *ballroom*, *festival*, *studio orchestra*.

Two of the four are usually enough. *Chopin style* became **Romantic-era salon nocturne**: era plus setting, and the piano, rubato and lyrical line are already elsewhere in the prompt. *Eno-style ambient* became **generative tape-loop ambient**: pure technique, which is what the name was pointing at in the first place.

## Three patterns worth stealing

- **Pairs of composers collapse into a school.** *Bach-Vivaldi style* → **High Baroque concerto style**. *Debussy-Ravel style* → **French Impressionist style**. When you need two names, you are describing a movement; name the movement.
- **Performers collapse into a technique.** *Tony Allen-style drums* → **polyrhythmic hi-hat-driven drumming**. *Beach Boys vocals* → **stacked falsetto harmonies**. Say what the hands and voices do.
- **Brands and events collapse into a context.** *Eurovision-style pop* → **pan-European contest pop**. *Disney-style dream ballad* → **musical-theatre dream ballad**. The context carries the same promise without the trademark.

## What stayed, and why

- **Rock in Opposition** stays in the Avant-Prog prompt. It is the name of a movement, not of a person or a band.
- **Motown Pop** stays as the *page name* of one style, because it is how people search for that sound. Its prompt no longer uses the word.
- Genre words that began as places or eras — *Weimar cabaret*, *Polynesian exotica*, *Soviet-era* — stay. They describe a sound, not who made it.
